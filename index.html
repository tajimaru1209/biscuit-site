How to Use

  1. Pick your buddy. Choose Biscuit the pup, Tofu the kitty, Clover the bunny or Maple the bear cub. You can switch anytime on the Buddy tab.
  2. Log a reading. Tap the pink + and enter your numbers on the big keypad. For blood pressure, add your pulse, arm and little notes like “Morning” or “After Meds.” For blood sugar, choose when you measured.
  3. See how it went. Your buddy reacts to each reading and shows its category. Blood pressure uses American Heart Association ranges; blood sugar uses the targets you set.
  4. Watch your trends. The Trends tab shows charts, averages and time in range for 7, 30 or 90 days.
  5. Share with your doctor. Tap Doctor Report to make a clean PDF, or export a spreadsheet (CSV).

FAQ

Biscuit is a logbook, not a medical device. It doesn’t measure your blood pressure or blood sugar and doesn’t give medical advice. Always follow your doctor’s guidance. In an emergency, call 911.

How do I earn treats?
Every reading you log counts toward the next treat for your buddy: a ribbon, a daisy, a beanie, a crown and more. Missing a day never takes anything away. Dress your buddy up on the Buddy tab.

What do the blood pressure categories mean?
Biscuit uses the American Heart Association ranges: Normal (below 120/80), Elevated (120–129 and below 80), High Stage 1 (130–139 or 80–89), High Stage 2 (140 or higher, or 90 or higher), and Very High (above 180 and/or above 120). Readings below 90/60 are shown as Low.

Can I change my blood sugar targets or units?
Yes. In Settings, choose mg/dL or mmol/L and set your lowest target, your before-meal target and your after-meal target. Use the numbers your doctor gave you.

Does Biscuit work with Apple Health?
Yes. Turn on “Save readings to Health” in Settings, and new readings are added to Apple Health. Biscuit only writes to Health; it never reads your Health data.

How do I edit or delete a reading?
Tap a reading in History to change it, or swipe left to delete it.

How do reminders work?
Add up to six daily reminders in Settings. Your buddy sends a friendly nudge at each time.

Which devices are supported?
iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Biscuit (“the App”) is provided by an independent developer (“we”). This policy explains how the App handles information.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. The App has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not send your data to any server.

Your readings
The blood pressure, pulse and blood sugar readings and notes you enter are stored only on your device. We never see them, and they are never uploaded or shared with us or anyone else.

Apple Health
If you turn on Apple Health in Settings, the App saves new readings to Apple Health on your device. The App does not read any data from Apple Health. Data the App writes to Apple Health is not used for advertising or sold, and is not shared with us or any third party. You can turn this off or change permissions anytime in the Health app.

Reports and exports
Doctor Reports (PDF) and spreadsheets (CSV) are created on your device. They are only shared when you choose to send them, using the share options you pick.

Reminders
Reminder notifications are scheduled on your device.

Purchase
The App is purchased through the App Store. Apple processes the payment, and we do not receive your name, email address or payment information. Please see Apple’s Privacy Policy for details.

Children’s Privacy

We do not collect, store or share any personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Deleting Your Data

You can delete any reading in History, or all readings in Settings → Delete All Readings. Deleting the App removes all of its data from your device. Readings saved to Apple Health can be removed in the Health app.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the App changes. Updates will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
